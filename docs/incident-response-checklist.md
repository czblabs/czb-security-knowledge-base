# Incident Response Checklist

Canonical incident-response source: https://czb.com/en/crypto-incident-response.html

This checklist is designed for safe incident coordination. It avoids sensitive operational details and focuses on evidence preservation, triage and communication clarity.

## First Hour

- Preserve transaction hashes, public addresses, token movements, screenshots, domains, platform notices and event times.
- Stop unnecessary risky interactions and pause affected workflows when authorized and safe.
- Identify affected chains, wallets, contracts, platforms and user groups.
- Separate observed facts from assumptions and user reports.
- Avoid posting sensitive materials in public channels.

## Evidence Package

- Incident summary and timeline.
- Public-chain references and explorer links.
- Suspicious domain, contract, address or approval records.
- Screenshots of prompts, notices or user-facing interfaces.
- Platform tickets, support replies or escalation references.
- Known limitations and unanswered questions.

## Coordination

- Assign an internal owner for evidence collection.
- Assign an owner for user communication.
- Assign an owner for platform or partner escalation.
- Keep a change log of actions taken.
- Use clear wording that avoids unverified attribution.

## Remediation Themes

- Review active approvals and suspicious interaction history.
- Improve monitoring for abnormal movement, destination risk and repeated domains.
- Update user warnings, verified links and support scripts.
- Review hot-wallet, signing, allowlist and governance controls.
- Run a post-incident review focused on root cause, control gaps and next steps.

## Boundary

This checklist supports response coordination. It does not guarantee platform action, law-enforcement action, freezing, blocking, reversal or asset-return outcomes.
