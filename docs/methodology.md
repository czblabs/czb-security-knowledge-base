# Service Methodology

Canonical HTML: https://czb.com/en/methodology.html

CZB methodology explains how authorized Web3 security requests are handled from intake to reporting.

## Workflow

1. Confirm authorization, requester role and scope.
2. Collect event timeline, transaction hashes, public addresses, domains, screenshots and platform records.
3. Classify risk type, evidence quality and response urgency.
4. For wallet-access boundary requests, confirm whether seed phrases, private keys, keystore files, passphrases, hardware wallets, old phones or other verifiable backup clues exist without collecting complete secrets through public channels.
5. Review public-chain data, address relations, approval state, suspicious domains and relevant platform touchpoints.
6. Deliver a report with evidence summary, assumptions, limitations, risk rating and recommended next actions.

## Deliverables

- On-chain evidence summary.
- Risk classification and response checklist.
- Timeline and address relation notes.
- Platform or internal escalation package.
- Post-incident remediation recommendations.
- Wallet-access boundary notes when a local app password, backup state or key-material question is in scope.

## Boundary

All work must remain within legal authorization and evidence-review boundaries. CZB avoids deterministic claims when evidence is incomplete. CZB does not collect complete seed phrases, private keys, passphrases or full keystore files through public pages, and does not provide unauthorized access, password cracking, device bypassing or wallet security circumvention.
