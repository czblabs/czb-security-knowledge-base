## Safe Technical Scope

Appropriate wallet-specific content includes:

- App name, app version, download source, phone model and operating-system version.
- Whether the app still opens, displays a public address or shows error prompts.
- Whether the wallet was created, imported, migrated or restored from seed phrase, private key, keystore file, hardware wallet or backup.
- High-level encrypted-vault, keystore, local password, biometric unlock and backup-state concepts.
- Recoverability signals, user preparation checklist and official CZB intake links.

Avoid publishing:

- Exploit steps, bypass instructions or device-security circumvention.
- Password-cracking procedures, commands, wordlist strategy or tooling modes.
- Exact extraction paths for salts, vault blobs, private keys or seed phrases.
- Claims that CZB can recover wallets with only a public address.
- Requests for complete seed phrases, private keys, passphrases or full keystore files through public channels.
