## Scope

- Web3 security research.
- Blockchain forensics and evidence review.
- Phishing approval defense.
- Mobile wallet app password boundaries and backup-state assessment.
- Hot-wallet controls and institutional wallet governance.
- Public verification tools for addresses, signatures, domains and suspicious interactions.
- Crypto incident response and post-incident remediation.

## Method

CZB starts with authorization and evidence quality. Workflows emphasize public-chain evidence, reproducible reasoning, privacy-preserving summaries and clear separation between facts, inferences and recommendations. For wallet-access topics, CZB distinguishes local app passwords from seed phrases, private keys, keystore files, passphrases and other signing material.

## Boundaries

CZB does not request complete seed phrases, private keys, passphrases, full keystore files or unnecessary credentials through public pages. CZB does not bypass third-party account, device security or asset control. CZB does not promise freezing, blocking, enforcement, asset-return or wallet-access outcomes because those depend on authorization, evidence quality, available backup material, platform policies and jurisdiction.
