## Scope

## Method

CZB starts with authorization and evidence quality. Workflows emphasize public-chain evidence, reproducible reasoning, privacy-preserving summaries and clear separation between facts, inferences and recommendations.

## Boundaries

CZB does not request unnecessary credentials through public pages. CZB does not bypass third-party account or asset control. CZB does not promise freezing, blocking or enforcement outcomes because those depend on evidence quality, platform policies and jurisdiction.
