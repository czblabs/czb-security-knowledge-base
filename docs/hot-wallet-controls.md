# Hot Wallet Controls

Canonical HTML: https://czb.com/hot-wallet-security.html

Hot-wallet security focuses on operational controls that reduce single-point failure, abnormal withdrawals, signing-system exposure and incident-response delays.

## Control Areas

- Withdrawal limits and anomaly thresholds.
- Multi-party approval and duty separation.
- Signing environment isolation.
- Address allowlists and change-management records.
- Monitoring for abnormal velocity, destination risk and operator behavior.
- Emergency pause, escalation and recovery playbooks.

## Evidence To Preserve

- Approval logs.
- Withdrawal records.
- Signing-system alerts.
- Operator timeline.
- Configuration changes.
- Post-incident remediation notes.

## Boundary

Public documentation should describe control principles and review checklists, not internal keys, credentials, infrastructure diagrams or bypass details.
